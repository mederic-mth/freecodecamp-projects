What is Karmine Corp ?

Karmine Corp (KC), is a French esports organization founded in 2020 by Kamel "Kameto" Kebir, a famous French streamer.

Known for its passionate community, the "Blue Wall," and its dominance in games like League of Legends, Valorant or Rocket League, KC has quickly risen to prominence.

With a focus on excellence, innovation, and fan engagement, the team has become a symbol of ambition and success in the esports world.
